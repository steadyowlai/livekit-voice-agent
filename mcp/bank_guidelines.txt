1. GENERAL COMPLIANCE & FAIR LENDING
- All lending decisions adhere to Equal Credit Opportunity and Fair Housing standards.
- Interest rates quoted are subject to underwriting verification, credit score validation,
  and debt-to-income (DTI) ratio remaining below 43%.

2. DISCLOSURES & CLOSING COSTS
- Retail mortgage estimates include standard origination fees (1.0%), title insurance,
  and escrow reserves for taxes/insurance.
- No prepayment penalties apply to retail residential or auto loan products.
- Commercial real estate and equipment financing may require environmental review and
  corporate guarantor financials.

3. REQUIRED BORROWER DOCUMENTATION
- Retail Borrowers: 2 most recent pay stubs, 2 years of W-2 forms, 2 months bank statements,
  and valid government-issued photo identification.
- Corporate Borrowers: 3 years audited business financial statements, tax returns,
  corporate resolution, and personal guarantees from principals holding >20% equity.

4. ESCALATION & BRANCH CONTACT
- For loan amounts exceeding standard thresholds ($750k retail / $10M corporate),
  escalate to Senior Underwriting Committee: underwriting@firstapexbank.com
- Customer Support Hotline: 1-800-555-APEX (Mon-Fri 8am-8pm EST)
